1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & 8-STEP ESCROW PAYMENT LIFECYCLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nutsumo connects Customers (Service Seekers) with Verified Artisans / Pros (Service Providers).
The platform operates on an Escrow Protection model (similar to Upwork / Fiverr) ensuring security
for both customers and artisans:

  Step 1: Customer accepts Artisan's quotation
  Step 2: Booking is created in status "AWAITING_PAYMENT" with Platform Fee & Net Payout calculated
  Step 3: Customer pays the full amount via Stripe Checkout before booking work begins
  Step 4: Payment succeeds -> Webhook updates paymentStatus to "ESCROW_HELD" & status to "CONFIRMED"
          (Funds are safely held in platform settlement account. Artisan is notified to begin work)
  Step 5: Artisan starts work ("IN_PROGRESS") and marks job as "DELIVERED" with notes & proof images
  Step 6: Customer inspects work and confirms completion ("COMPLETED") + leaves optional rating/feedback
  Step 7: Admin reviews completed booking and approves payout ("APPROVED")
  Step 8: Platform commission (10%) is deducted and remaining net funds are released to Artisan ("RELEASED")

Status Enums:
  - Job Status: 'OPEN' | 'BOOKED' | 'IN_PROGRESS' | 'COMPLETED' | 'CANCELLED'
  - Quotation Status: 'PENDING' | 'ACCEPTED' | 'REJECTED' | 'WITHDRAWN'
  - Booking Status: 'AWAITING_PAYMENT' | 'CONFIRMED' | 'IN_PROGRESS' | 'DELIVERED' | 'COMPLETED' | 'CANCELLED'
  - Booking Payment Status: 'UNPAID' | 'ESCROW_HELD' | 'PAID' | 'REFUNDED'
  - Booking Payout Status: 'PENDING' | 'APPROVED' | 'RELEASED' | 'REJECTED'


====================================================================================================
2. ESCROW & FINANCIAL BREAKDOWN
====================================================================================================
Platform Commission Rate: 10% (Default)

Example:
  Quotation Proposed Amount (Total): $150.00
  Platform Commission (10%):           $15.00  (Retained by Platform as fee)
  Artisan Payout Amount (Net):        $135.00  (Released to Artisan upon completion & admin approval)

When Customer pays:
  Customer pays full $150.00 -> Held in Platform Escrow (paymentStatus: 'ESCROW_HELD')
When Admin releases payout:
  $15.00 Platform Fee is retained -> $135.00 is disbursed to Artisan (payoutStatus: 'RELEASED')

PAGE C1: "POST A JOB" (Create Service Request)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method & URL: POST /api/v1/job
Content-Type: multipart/form-data
Headers: Authorization: Bearer <CustomerToken>

Form Data Fields:
  - title: "Fix Leaking Kitchen Sink" (string, min 3, max 100)
  - description: "The sink in the master kitchen is leaking under the pipe..." (string, min 10)

  - service: "6ae165f30bd4d47acdc57fcd" (ObjectId string)
  - location: "Manhattan, New York, NY" (string)
  - postCode: "10001" (string)
  - budget: 150 (number)
  - preferredDate: "2026-11-15T10:00:00.000Z" (ISO date string)
