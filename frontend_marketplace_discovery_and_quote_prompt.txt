SECTION 1: DISCOVERY FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE & PAGE HIERARCHY
====================================================================================================

The user journey consists of a 4-step progressive funnel:

  [Page 1: Home Page] 
         │ (Customer clicks on a Category, e.g. "Plumbing")
         ▼
  [Page 2: Category Services Showcase] 
         │ (Customer clicks on a specific Service, e.g. "Pipe Leak Repair")
         ▼
  [Page 3: Service Artisans Listing Page] 
         │ (Customer filters by location/rating and clicks on an Artisan card)
         ▼
  [Page 4: Artisan 360° Public Profile] 
         │ (Customer reviews Bio, Portfolio, Stats, Reviews, and clicks "Request Custom Quote")
         ▼
  [Page 5: Direct Quote Modal & Escrow Order Lifecycle]

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 1: HOME PAGE (`/`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key UI Sections:
1. Hero Search: Search input ("Find plumbers, electricians...") + Location/Postcode input.
2. Category Grid / Carousel:
   - Displays all active categories with icon/image and name.
   - Clicking a category navigates to `/categories/${category._id}` (or slug).
   - API: `GET /api/v1/category?isActive=true`
3. Trust Strip: "Verified Professionals • Escrow Protected Payments • Satisfaction Guarantee".
4. Top Rated Artisans: Carousel of 5-star artisans with verified badges.
   - API: `GET /api/v1/artisan-profile?sort=-rating&limit=6`

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 2: CATEGORY SERVICES SHOWCASE (`/categories/:id`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Displays all specialized service types under the chosen category (e.g. Under "Plumbing" -> Pipe Repair,
Drain Cleaning, Water Heater, Sink Installation).
Key UI Sections:
1. Category Header: Name, banner image, and description.
2. Services Grid:
   - Each card shows: Service image, title, short description, and "Browse Pros" CTA.
   - Clicking a service navigates to `/services/${service._id}/pros`.
   - API: `GET /api/v1/service?category=${categoryId}&isActive=true`

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 3: SERVICE ARTISANS LISTING PAGE (`/services/:id/pros` or `/search`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Displays all verified professionals offering the selected service.
Key UI Sections:
1. Filter & Sort Bar:
   - Location / Postcode filter
   - Availability filter (`Available` vs `Booked`)
   - Rating filter (4.5★+, 4.0★+)
   - Price Sort (Lowest price, Highest rated, Most completed jobs)
2. Artisan Result Cards:
   - Avatar & Verified Pro Badge (blue checkmark if `artisan.verified === true`)
   - Name & Business Name (e.g., "David Miller • Miller Plumbing Pros")
   - Rating & Reviews count: "★ 4.9 (42 reviews)"
   - Completed Jobs badge: "✓ 35 jobs completed"
   - Rate display: "$35/hr" or "Starting from $50"
   - Location & distance
   - Action Buttons:
     * "View Profile" -> navigates to `/pro/${artisan._id}`
     * "Request Quote" -> opens the Direct Quote Modal immediately
   - API: `GET /api/v1/artisan-service?service=${serviceId}&isActive=true`

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 4: ARTISAN 360° PUBLIC PROFILE (`/pro/:userId`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A complete trust-building profile answering every customer hesitation:

Component 1: Hero Identity Card
- Large Avatar, Verified Identity Badge, Business Name, Location, Postcode.
- Status Pill: `🟢 Available for Hire` or `🟡 Currently Booked`.
- Primary CTAs: **"Request Custom Quote"** (Primary Button) and "Message".

Component 2: Key Metric Stats Strip
- Star Rating: `4.9 ★` (from `profile.rating`)
- Total Reviews: `48 Reviews` (from `profile.reviewsCount`)
- Completed Jobs: `52 Completed` (from `profile.completedJobs`)
- Trade Experience: `8+ Years` (from `profile.experienceYears`)
- Starting Rate: `$35/hr` (from `profile.hourlyRate` or `startingRate`)
- API: `GET /api/v1/artisan-profile/user/${userId}`

Component 3: About & Credentials
- Detailed Bio, specialized skills, tools used, certifications.

Component 4: Services Offered (Catalog)
- Table/List of individual services this artisan offers with their exact custom rate:
  * "Pipe Leak Repair" | $35/hr | [Request Quote]
  * "Bathroom Sink Installation" | $50 fixed | [Request Quote]
- API: `GET /api/v1/artisan-service?artisan=${userId}&isActive=true`

Component 5: Interactive Portfolio (Before & After Showcase)
- Gallery cards showing prior work.
- Tagged with labels: `Before`, `After`, and `Work`.
- Includes an interactive slider or side-by-side comparison.
- API: `GET /api/v1/portfolio/artisan/${userId}`

Component 6: Ratings & Client Reviews
- Summary Box: Average rating (4.9/5) + Rating distribution bars (5★, 4★, 3★, 2★, 1★).
- Review Cards:
  * Customer name, avatar, and date.
  * Verified Job Title badge: "Reviewed for: Fix Leaking Kitchen Sink".
  * Star rating & client review comment.
  * Artisan Reply Box (if artisan responded to feedback).
- API: `GET /api/v1/review/artisan/${userId}`


====================================================================================================
SECTION 2: METHOD 1 — "REQUEST CUSTOM QUOTE" WORKFLOW (STEP-BY-STEP)
====================================================================================================

This is the recommended Fiverr Custom Offer / Upwork Direct Proposal workflow:

Step 1: Customer clicks "Request Custom Quote" on Artisan Profile
- A clean modal opens with the Artisan's identity pinned at the top:
  "Request a Custom Quote from [Artisan Name]".
- Form Fields:
  * Service (Dropdown pre-selected if clicked from a specific service row)
  * Project Title: (e.g. "Repair master bathroom pipe leak")
  * Detailed Description: (Explain what is needed, min 10 chars)
  * Location / Address & Postcode
  * Preferred Date & Time: (Date picker)
  * Estimated Budget ($): (Optional / Customer's expectation)
  * Upload Photos of the Issue: (Multipart file upload, up to 10 photos)

Step 2: Submit Direct Job Request to Backend
- Method & URL: `POST /api/v1/job`
- Content-Type: `multipart/form-data`
- Headers: `Authorization: Bearer <CustomerToken>`
- Payload:
  * `title`: "Repair master bathroom pipe leak"
  * `description`: "Water is dripping under the cabinet..."
  * `category`: "<categoryId>"
  * `service`: "<serviceId>"
  * `selectedArtisan`: "<artisanUserId>"  <--- PINS JOB DIRECTLY TO THIS ARTISAN
  * `location`: "123 Main St, New York, NY"
  * `postCode`: "10001"
  * `budget`: 120
  * `preferredDate`: "2026-11-20T10:00:00.000Z"
  * `images`: [File, File]

Step 3: Artisan Receives Direct Notification
- Backend automatically triggers an In-App Notification and Kafka Event to the artisan:
  "New Direct Job Inquiry! 🎯: A customer requested a custom quote for 'Repair master bathroom pipe leak'."
- Artisan views job details at `/pro/jobs/:jobId`.

Step 4: Artisan Submits Official Quotation / Offer
- Artisan enters their proposed pricing and timeline.
- Method & URL: `POST /api/v1/quotation`
- Headers: `Authorization: Bearer <ArtisanToken>`
- Payload:
  ```json
  {
    "job": "<jobId>",
    "proposedAmount": 140,
    "rateType": "FIXED",
    "estimatedDuration": "2 hours",
    "message": "I can bring replacement copper seals and complete this tomorrow at 10 AM."
  }
  ```

Step 5: Customer Reviews Quotation with 20% Statutory Breakdown
- Customer receives notification: "Artisan sent you a quotation for $140.00".
- Customer navigates to `/account/jobs/:jobId/proposals`.
- Proposal card displays transparent breakdown:
  * Proposed Amount:           $140.00
  * VAT (15%):                 -$21.00
  * NHIL (2.5%):                -$3.50
  * GETFund Levy (2.5%):        -$3.50
  * Total Platform Deductions: -$28.00
  * Artisan Net Payout:        $112.00
  * Customer Pays:             $140.00 (Escrow Protected)

Step 6: Customer Accepts Quotation & Pays via Stripe Escrow
- Customer clicks "Accept & Pay Escrow".
- Method & URL: `PATCH /api/v1/quotation/:quotationId/accept`
- Headers: `Authorization: Bearer <CustomerToken>`
- Response (200 OK):
  ```json
  {
    "success": true,
    "message": "Quotation accepted successfully. Please complete payment to confirm booking.",
    "data": {
      "booking": {
        "_id": "6b02f89a45c428cac7e80fa5",
        "bookingNumber": "BK-280fa5-4819",
        "status": "AWAITING_PAYMENT",
        "totalAmount": 140
      },
      "checkoutUrl": "https://checkout.stripe.com/c/pay/cs_test_..."
    }
  }
  ```
- Frontend immediately redirects customer to `checkoutUrl`.

Step 7: Payment Success & Order In-Progress
- Stripe webhook confirms payment -> Booking moves to `CONFIRMED` (`paymentStatus: ESCROW_HELD`).
- Artisan receives "Escrow Secured! 💰" notification and starts work (`IN_PROGRESS`).

SECTION 3: FRONTEND API CHEAT-SHEET
====================================================================================================

| Action / Screen | HTTP Method & URL | Auth Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Get Active Categories | `GET /api/v1/category?isActive=true` | No | Home Page category list |

| Get Service Artisans | `GET /api/v1/artisan-service?service=:id&isActive=true` | No | Artisans providing service |
| Get Artisan Profile | `GET /api/v1/artisan-profile/user/:userId` | No | Bio, stats, rating, experience |
| Get Artisan Services | `GET /api/v1/artisan-service?artisan=:userId&isActive=true` | No | Custom rates per service |
| Get Artisan Portfolio | `GET /api/v1/portfolio/artisan/:userId` | No | Before/After work photos |
| Get Artisan Reviews | `GET /api/v1/review/artisan/:userId` | No | Reviews, ratings & 5★ breakdown |
| Submit Direct Quote Req| `POST /api/v1/job` (with `selectedArtisan`) | Customer | Customer requests quote |
| Artisan Submits Quote | `POST /api/v1/quotation` | Artisan | Artisan sends proposed price |
| Accept Quote & Checkout| `PATCH /api/v1/quotation/:id/accept` | Customer | Creates booking & returns Stripe URL |
| Get Booking Details | `GET /api/v1/booking/:id` | Customer/Artisan | Realtime booking status & escrow info |
